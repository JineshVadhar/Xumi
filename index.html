[ 03 // FROM THE WORKBENCH ] NOTE FROM VELQORA LABS

Why We Built Xumi

Like a lot of developers, designers, and creators, we spend 8 to 12 hours a day sitting behind monitors. Most desk accessories are either completely static figurines, or they're aggressive smart screens that bomb you with phone notifications, calendar alerts, and digital noise.

We built Xumi because we wanted something quiet, tactile, and genuinely charming. An object that feels like a pet on your desk—one that sleeps when your room is dark, perks up when you tap its head between deep work sessions, and never asks you for a subscription or an app login.

Every animation was hand-drawn frame-by-frame on OLED dot-matrix grids, and each prototype was tested right here on our workbench. If you're passionate about robotics, desktop aesthetics, or want to reserve a unit from Batch 01, we'd love to hear from you.

[ 04 // HARDWARE SPEC SHEET ]

Detailed Specifications

Precision engineering packed into an ultra-compact desktop footprint.

DISPLAY & OPTICS
1.12" Monochrome OLED
128 × 64 resolution, 60 FPS refresh, wide 170° viewing angle, deep contrast.
INTERACTION SENSORS
Capacitive Touch Array
Dual-zone crown and cheek sensing. Detects taps, long presses, and soothing strokes.
CORE PROCESSOR
32-bit Low-Power SoC
Dedicated finite state machine engine with non-volatile memory for personality persistence.
BATTERY & POWER
USB-C Fast Recharge
High-density Li-Po cell. Up to 14 hours active companion mode or 5+ days in deep sleep.
AUDIO SYSTEM
Resonant Acoustic Chamber
Soft emotive tones, playful chirps, and calming breathing hums. Easily muted via touch.
CHASSIS & MATERIALS
Matte ABS & Silicone Ring
Weight: 118 grams. Dimensions: 62mm × 58mm × 55mm. Weighted anti-slip desk footing.
